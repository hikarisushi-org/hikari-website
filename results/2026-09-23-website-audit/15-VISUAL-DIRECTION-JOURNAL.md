## Assistant interpretations — hypotheses for Joel to validate

### Confirmed component preference (distinct from interpretations below)

Joel supplied a Saffron Valley gallery screenshot and said “liked this a lot.” Strong positive reference: six large food photographs in a three-column/two-row grid, with no dish descriptions or prices within the pictured gallery. Assistant interpretation: close food framing, visible textures, and uninterrupted photography create appetite appeal. The specific reason for the strong like was not isolated by Joel; do not turn the exact six-image count, grid dimensions, rounded corners, or dark background into requirements. This strengthens the gallery as a design candidate without authorizing implementation or approving the earlier preview. The screenshot is Saffron, not the ambient CAVA tab.

Joel supplied a Pokeworks screenshot of Signature Works and Signature Drinks and explicitly said: “I like this layout of nice picture on one side and text and cta on the other.” Record the split photo/text/CTA component as a confirmed preference. The screenshot alternates image sides between sections, but alternation, exact section count, orange buttons, typography, rounded corners, and mobile treatment were not individually approved. This does not authorize implementation or retroactively approve the earlier preview.

1. **Visual ease may create confidence before interaction.** Joel's Pokeworks reaction suggests he wants visitors to anticipate an uncomplicated ordering process, not just admire a beautiful page.
2. **Let food provide most of the color.** Light neutral surfaces, generous spacing, and a restrained accent could deliver brightness without a loud palette. This is an interpretation of his color feedback, not an approved palette.
3. **Make the primary action unmistakable.** A visible, consistent Order Online action near the top-right on desktop is worth preserving as a candidate. Mobile placement needs its own design/proof; do not infer a specific sticky treatment has been approved.
4. **Maman supplies warmth; Pokeworks supplies clarity.** Current synthesis: Pokeworks' clear food-led structure and apparent ease, with Maman's warmth/lightness, expressed in a modern Japanese identity. Joel has liked the references but has not approved this synthesis as a final design.
5. **Modern femininity need not mean pink or flowers.** Soft daylight, graceful proportions, restrained details and welcoming photography are possible expressions. Exact treatments remain open.
6. **Food must remain central.** Lifestyle, chef, and restaurant imagery may support it, but the site should clearly promise an appetizing meal and an easy way to order.
7. **Calm should not feel lifeless.** The combination of Just Salad's wrong-kind-of-bright colors and BIBIBOP feeling too static suggests a light, restrained direction that still has energy. This is an assistant interpretation; whether that energy should come from photography, layout rhythm, or subtle motion is not yet established.
8. **Appetizing food is the photography criterion, not background brightness.** The assistant initially attributed Bamboo's negative impression to its dark surroundings and suggested lighter settings. Joel corrected that emphasis with positive Din Tai Fung food photos. Visible texture, close framing, and clearly lit food are possible contributors in the supplied positive example (assistant interpretation, not individually confirmed preferences). A darker page or photographic setting is not automatically disqualifying. The overall light/positive brand direction remains separate from this photography judgment.

# Square Online and delivery-app item parity — September 23, 2026

Joel's target is the **same orderable items** on Square Online, DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub. The baseline is the current Hikari website menu, subject to the owner-confirmed exclusions: Sunrise Salad and Sweet Summer Salad are not offered, and Mochi (4 Pcs) must not be ordered online. Their website listings remain for now at Joel's request. Prices, offers, fees, modifiers, and hours were not part of this parity request.

## Changes in Square

The three delivery apps are connected and active in Square, using the Dine-In Menu. In the Square item editor, the `Delivery apps` group selects DoorDash, Grubhub, and Uber Eats together.

Enabled all three delivery-app channels for these 14 website-matched items that lacked them:

- Spicy Tuna; Spicy Salmon; Philly; Spicy Hamachi; Veggie Roll; Sweet Potato Roll
- Cowboy; Jalapeño Bomb; Sashimi Platter; Mango Sticky Rice; Strawberry Spritz
- Fire Spicy Tuna; Playboy; Hikari Delight

Disabled delivery-app channels for nine items not sold on the current website menu: Diamond, Lava Volcano Roll, Mt Fuji Roll, Rainbow Roll, Cali Mex Roll, Hikari Fire Crunch, Butterflied Garlic Shrimp (6), Red Ruby, and Maki. The first six and Garlic Shrimp had been hidden from Square Online earlier, but were still visible to the apps. The Square catalog records and Points of sale channel remain; nothing was deleted.

Removed stray `Online – Website & Profile` visibility from Red Ruby, Maki, Cowboy Crunch, Misc. Item, Sunrise Salad, and Sweet Summer Salad. Mochi, Hot Tea, and Extra fish were already POS-only. The matching Square catalog rows, including six branded canned sodas representing the website's Canned Soda entry, were reviewed for channel coverage.
